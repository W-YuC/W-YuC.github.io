I am **Yuchong Wu**, a senior undergraduate student at the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/), where I am pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science.

My research interests lie in the post-training of large language models (LLM) and the application of autonomous agents across various domains. Specifically, I am passionate about:

*   **LLM Post-training**: Enhancing LLM's reasoning capabilities through techniques like RLVR and OPD.
*   **LLM Agents**: Building and evaluating LLM-based agents that can perform complex, multi-step tasks in real-world scenarios, with a focus on tool use and reasoning.

I am actively seeking **Ph.D. positions for Fall 2027** to continue my research journey.
